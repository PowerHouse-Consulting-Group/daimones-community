**Location:** Greece / Singapore (PowerHouse Consulting Group Pte Ltd)

## 1. Campaign Framing

### Project Story

daïmōnes is a sovereign AI for uncensored philosophical reasoning. It is built on the Corpus Aristotelicum and runs without corporate guardrails.

This Kickstarter funds three things:
1. **Corpus expansion** — add Plato, Stoics, and Kant to the existing Aristotle corpus.
2. **Real-time voice interaction** — speak with the Oracle, not just type.
3. **PWA app** — take daïmōnes offline-capable on mobile and desktop.

Backers get lifetime access, early personas, and a direct role in shaping the platform.

### Why Kickstarter?
- Validate B2C demand before committing heavy dev time.
- Build a paid backer community before Product Hunt launch.
- Fund GPU and storage costs for corpus expansion and voice infrastructure.

## 7. Risks & Compliance

### Kickstarter Rules
- Rewards must not be equity, financial instruments, or lottery.
- Lifetime digital access is allowed if defined as access to the service.
- Hardware rewards are not being offered.
- Creator must be 18+ and have a bank account in an eligible country.

### Potential Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| No existing audience | Promote heavily on Reddit/X; email list from daimones.ai |
| Voice feature delay | Ship async voice first; communicate transparently |
| Funding misses goal | Set $5k as realistic minimum; $10k as stretch |
| Chargebacks | Clear reward terms; deliver digital goods promptly |
