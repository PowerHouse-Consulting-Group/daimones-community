## Production Notes

- Generate all base images at highest available resolution, then resize/crop to target dimensions.
- For text overlays, use PIL/Pillow (Python) or HTML/SVG → PNG pipeline. Never rely on the image generator for text.
- All overlays should use the same font family for brand consistency (recommend: Inter or Space Grotesk for body, a geometric sans for wordmark).
- Cyan hex: #00E5FF. Deep blue hex: #0A1628. Marble warm highlight: #D4C5A9.
- Export final assets as PNG (transparency where needed) and WebP (for web delivery).
- Product Hunt gallery accepts up to 5 images + 1 video. Recommended gallery set: #1 (hero), #3 (OG/Lyceum), #4 (pricing), #5 (comparison), #10 (demo preview).
- Remaining images (#2, #6, #7, #8, #9) for social media promotion, press kit, and pre-launch teasers.
