## Hugging Face
We run the public demo of daimones on an HF Space. The Space is a thin Gradio proxy to our own sovereign VM, so visitors can talk to Aristotle in two clicks while the RAG corpus and persona prompts never leave our infrastructure. For a solo founder, it is the cheapest way to give the world a live demo that never sleeps.

## Cloudflare
Cloudflare protects daimones.ai from day one: DNS, caching, and Turnstile on our lead-capture forms without a single CAPTCHA that annoys real humans. Turnstile alone saved us from the bot signups every small AI product gets in its first week. Free tier, and it behaves like enterprise gear.

## Stripe
Stripe powers the Disciple and Archon subscriptions on daimones.ai. Payment links meant I had working paid tiers before I had a logo finalized, and the webhooks made entitlement checks almost boring. Boring is exactly what you want from payments when you are a one-man team.

## Directus
Our whole blog runs on self-hosted Directus: 40+ articles in two languages, server-rendered pages, and an API our cron jobs publish to directly. Because it is self-hosted, the content pipeline is sovereign end to end, which is the same principle our product is built on. It is a CMS that does not fight you.

## Hashnode
Hashnode is our public blog mirror, and it punched far above its weight: the follow links from daimones.hashnode.dev were the main driver of our first real domain rating jump. Cross-posting with canonical URLs back to daimones.ai took minutes to set up. For an independent AI project, it is free distribution on a domain search engines already trust.
