## 1. What daïmōnes is (in one minute)

daïmōnes is a sovereign AI reasoning platform for organizations that analyze sensitive, contested, or politically charged topics. It runs on infrastructure you control, reasons transparently from sources you choose, and refuses to sanitize conclusions to match a corporate acceptable-use policy. Every output is auditable, attributable, and under your editorial control.

## 3. First-tier questions and answers

### What makes daïmōnes different from ChatGPT, Claude, or enterprise AI assistants?

Commercial AI platforms are optimized for broad acceptability. daïmōnes is optimized for intellectual honesty. It does not have a corporate moderation layer, it runs on your infrastructure, and it cites the documents it uses.

### Is it safe for politically sensitive work?

Safety here means traceability, not silence. daïmōnes does not hide controversial conclusions; it documents the reasoning and sources behind them. Editorial responsibility sits with your organization, exactly as it does for any other research product.

### Can it be deployed without sending data to third parties?

Yes. Sovereign deployment keeps all data, queries, and outputs inside your environment. This is essential for sources, sources, whistleblower material, or any information that must not be exposed to external APIs.

### How does it handle AI-generated content and transparency?

The platform labels AI-generated outputs with provenance metadata and can embed machine-readable markers. This helps your organization disclose when content is AI-assisted, satisfying emerging transparency expectations and EU AI Act Article 50 obligations.

### Does it watermark AI-generated reports or images?

Yes. daïmōnes can attach provenance and watermarking metadata to AI-generated text, charts, and images. For NGOs and think tanks publishing public reports, this creates a clear record of which parts were human-written, AI-assisted, or fully AI-generated.

### What does it cost?

Policy and civil-society pricing is scaled to organization size and deployment complexity. Contact architect@daimones.ai for a tailored quote; nonprofit and multi-year discounts are available.

### How fast can we deploy?

A pilot deployment typically takes 2-4 weeks after corpus review and infrastructure assessment. A limited sandbox can often be made available sooner for evaluation.

### Who owns the data and outputs?

Your organization owns everything. We do not train on your data, and we cannot access your outputs in a sovereign deployment.

### How does this fit with the EU AI Act?

daïmōnes supports the AI Act's transparency and human-oversight requirements. Deployers can generate audit logs, label AI content, and maintain human-in-the-loop review — especially important for high-impact publications, grant reports, and public-facing advocacy materials.

---

## 5. What not to say

- Do not claim daïmōnes is "unregulated." It is compliant-by-design and supports human oversight.
- Do not promise it will never produce errors. Promise that errors are inspectable and correctable.
- Do not position it as a tool for disinformation. It is a transparency tool for legitimate research.
- Avoid inflammatory or conspiratorial framing. Keep the conversation institutional, technical, and evidence-based.
